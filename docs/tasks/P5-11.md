- Already closed in Phase 4: `crates/ui` has the `proptest` dev-dependency (P4-16), `crates/ui-spec/fuzz/.gitignore` has `!corpus/`, and the `crates/ui/fuzz` seeds are in per-target directories (P4-17). - The undo property must compare the **reachable** state (the pages, the shapes reachable from them, their sheets, text and `Connect` records, and the masters that instances use), not `Document` equality. Group, insert and drop undo leave detached arena entries behind by design, and the first drop of a catalog master leaves the materialised master in the document (P5-06 open issue). Compare those as known differences, and list them in the report.
- Selection and tool choice are not edits: `edit.select` and the `tools.*` commands add no undo step and do not bump `changes` (P5-16, P5-17). Re-routing after an edit is not undoable on its own (ADR 0045); compare paths after undo, not the re-route count.
