## Goal

Point lookup against the scene (selection, glue, handles) and the
overlay mark builders, with one deterministic implementation the UI and
the tests share.

## Interfaces

Implement the stubs exactly as signed:
`geom::{distance_point_segment, distance_point_path, distance_point_rect,
path_contains, snap_point, snap_axis, snap_grid}`,
`render::{hit_test, hit_marquee, node_bounds, scene_bounds,
build_selection_overlay}`, and record `shape_id` in `build.rs` (the
builder's shape construction site). Consumers (do not edit): P5-05
(`crates/ui`), P5-08 (glue candidates), P5-10/P5-11 (tests).

Rules the bodies must follow (ADR 0041): results topmost first, filled
shapes hit on fill and outline, unfilled shapes on outline and text,
`distance` in inches, marquee by bounds intersection. Geometry math goes
through `geom` (L0); `render` applies scene policy.
