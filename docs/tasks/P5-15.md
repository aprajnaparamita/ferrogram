## Goal

1. Test every uncovered branch of `mutate.rs`. Cover the error arms
   especially: unknown shape, page or master ids, `WouldCycle`,
   `NotOnPage`, `BadCell`, `TooManyShapes`, and an empty or mixed-page
   group list. Also cover the edge cases of `scale_geometry`, z-order
   at the ends of the list, page delete or move with backgrounds,
   ungroup with connects, and `next_visio_id` near its cap.
2. Assert behavior, not just reachability. Each test checks the
   documented result or invariant from ADR 0039: ids stay stable, no
   dangling `Connects`, and backgrounds follow their page.
3. Cover the 10 lines in `lookup.rs` if the inputs reach them.
4. Report any bug a test finds, and fix it with a regression test.
