### Deviations from the Interfaces section

- The fixture builders are `build(Fixture::Feature(Feature::...))` (the
  `Fixture` enum gained one variant) rather than a bare
  `build(Feature(..))` call: every fixture consumer (`path`,
  `file_name`, `COMMITTED_FIXTURES`, the regenerate test) works on
  `Fixture`, and one enum keeps them uniform.
- The golden comparison decodes PNGs with `tiny_skia` (already a
  dependency); no new crate was added for it. The metric compares
  demultiplied RGBA channels.
- The oracle comparison reads the XHTML through its own small parser
  (`d`-attribute path data in the open SVG vocabulary: `M`, `L`, `Z`,
  `A`; anything else records a skip). Bounding boxes are compared in
  absolute page coordinates (both sides agreed on page size for every
  compared page); our box samples curves at 16 points per segment and
  the oracle's arcs at 33 points per arc, both far inside the 0.05 in
  tolerance.
- `crates/render/Cargo.toml` gained `insta` and `proptest` as
  dev-dependencies (P3-05 owns that file; unavoidable for the ADR 0027
  snapshot and property tests). `Cargo.lock` is committed with them.

### Open issues

- **Oracle comparison scope** (ADR 0027 "where it is meaningful"): 3
  of 15 corpus pages take the full per-page comparison today. The
  recorded skip reasons: our scene carries text (the oracle draws text
  as extra paths and groups, inflating its counts and box), group
  shapes (the two renderers flatten groups differently), or a shape
  whose fill or stroke fell back unresolved on our side (themed paints,
  a P3-15 open item: `dwg`, `fdo86664`, `testfile5` render with no
  resolved paint). `testfile1` and `testfile5` also show our geometry
  box smaller than the oracle's on arcs/ellipses — likely the `Ellipse`
  oblique case P3-01 flagged and the master-inherited geometry issue
  P3-15 left open. When those are fixed, tighten
  `skip_reason` in `tests/oracle_render.rs` and the compared set grows
  without code changes.
- The two known-malformed corpus files (`recursion-cycle.vsdx`,
  `tab-short-prefix.vsdx`) are named in `tests/oracle_render.rs`
  (`KNOWN_MALFORMED`): the oracle emits an empty or duplicate page for
  them, so only "no panic" is comparable there.
- `crates/vsdx/tests/fixtures.rs` hardcodes its own lists in
  `every_variant_builds_deterministically` and `file_names_are_distinct`
  (13 variants); the 11 feature variants are not in those two tests
  (the file belongs to P2-08/P2-09). Determinism and byte equality of
  the new fixtures are still enforced by
  `committed_fixtures_match_the_builder_output` through
  `COMMITTED_FIXTURES`. Fold the new variants into those lists in the
  next fix task.
- Golden regeneration prints its changed-file list through `println!`;
  libtest captures it for passing tests, so the list shows only with
  `-- --nocapture` (documented in `tests/golden.rs`).
- Linux golden drift may need the metric loosened (P3-01 known issue);
  the metric constants are the two at the top of `tests/golden.rs`.
- The text fixture's tab stop and bullet rendering is approximate
  (P3-05 open items: `tp` rows and multi-stop tab rows are ignored,
  bullets use our own symbols). The fixture pins current behaviour.
