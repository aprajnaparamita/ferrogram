## Goal

1. Test the uncovered branches of `connector.rs`, `insert.rs`,
   `shape.rs`, `edit.rs` and `view.rs` through the registry, the same

   Error arms first: bad or missing arguments, unknown ids, no document,
   no page, a stale selection.

   a failed command leaves the document and the undo stack unchanged,
   and undo restores the model exactly.
3. Leave margin: aim for **98.4%** or higher, so the next handler change
   does not drop the crate under the floor again.
