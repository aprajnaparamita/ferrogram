## Note from the master

- ADR 0038 rule 3: `scale_geometry` always materialises the row union into the instance's own `Geometry` section before scaling. A resized instance therefore draws at its new size even when the master's rows use formulas such as `Width*0.5`. The ADR's sentence about such masters "degrading to master coordinates" covers only instances that were never resized. Test it with one of our own P4-06 masters.
