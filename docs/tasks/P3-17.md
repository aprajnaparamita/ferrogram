## Goal

Cover the five major findings of the coverage audit and the minor ones
that are cheap:

1. Render builder fallbacks for missing and wrong-kind cells (number,
   bool, colour, row number, `LayerMember`, geometry section, `Text`).
2. PNG hostile-input guards: non-finite, sub-pixel and oversize pixmaps;
   degenerate gradients; the device-space bound added by the master for
   P3-14 F4 (`fits_device`).
3. SVG output cap (`Error::TooLarge`), quad and cubic segment mapping, and
   the first-segment direction for line ends.
4. PNG line ends: start end, colour override, filled and outlined bodies.
5. CLI usage errors (unexpected argument, unknown option) and the warning
   report text for every `WarningKind` and `CellValue` kind.
6. Minor 1–5 where a test is cheap: the text `nominal()` fallback, font
   edge cases, style-level warnings, `InvalidStyleData` on duplicate
   indices, PNG pattern tiles, quad/cubic dashes and a second clip.
7. Phase 2 carry-overs (minor 6–9) in `crates/vsdx/src/write/` and
   `package/read.rs`: the writer's `?` error arms and
   `refresh_content_types` adding a missing `Override`.

Leave the by-design defensive fallbacks (minor 10) alone.
